## The Speed Challenge: Why 72 Hours Seemed Impossible

For decades, the manufacturing industry accepted **15-25 day lead times** as inevitable. The conventional wisdom was simple: *"Good, Fast, Cheap—pick two."*

**Traditional Manufacturing Timeline Reality**:
- ⏰ **3-5 days**: Quote and order processing
- 📋 **2-4 days**: Material procurement and receiving
- 🔧 **1-3 days**: Programming and tooling preparation
- ⚙️ **5-8 days**: Production scheduling and machining
- 🔍 **2-3 days**: Quality control and inspection
- 📦 **1-2 days**: Packaging and shipping coordination
- **Total**: 14-25 days minimum

**But this model was built on outdated assumptions**:
1. **Sequential Processing**: Each step waits for the previous to complete
2. **Batch Manufacturing**: Small orders wait behind large production runs
3. **Manual Coordination**: Human-dependent scheduling and communication
4. **Reactive Quality Control**: Inspection happens after problems occur
5. **Supplier Dependencies**: Waiting for external vendors and subcontractors

At Geppetto, we threw out these assumptions and built a **parallel processing, AI-coordinated system** that consistently delivers in 72 hours without quality compromises.
