## Program Context

The customer needed a structural prototype to validate topology-optimized designs under aggressive timelines. Traditional vendors quoted 30 days just for CAM programming.

## Manufacturing Obstacles

- Large-format freeform surfaces with intersecting ribs.
- Thin walls created vibration and deformation risks.
- Raw billet (15 kg) had to be reduced to 1.2 kg finished weight.
- 5-axis programs would normally take weeks to perfect.

## Geppetto Solution

1. **AI CAM core:** Our system recognized features automatically, produced collision-free multi-axis paths, and predicted cycle times.
2. **Material strategy:** Intelligent stock planning and adaptive roughing increased utilization and reduced scrap.
3. **Quality supervision:** Senior machinists monitored vibration and thermal drift while CMM checkpoints kept geometry in spec.

## Outcome

- **Lead time:** Quote in 8h, machining and finishing completed within 72h.
- **Precision:** Surface deviation stayed within ±0.01 mm, matching structural analysis needs.
- **Data package:** Delivered detailed machining logs, QA reports, and optimization notes for the customer’s digital thread.

## Business Impact

The aerospace team proved manufacturability before entering wind-tunnel testing, saving weeks of schedule risk and providing concrete data for executive buy-in.***
