## Pitfall #1: The Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) Cash Drain

### 💀 Traditional Trap Scenario
"We only need 20 prototypes for testing, but the factory demands 500 minimum order. We have to bite the bullet..."

**Real Case**: A Shenzhen robotics startup needed 20 test samples but was forced to order 500 structural components, tying up $22,000 in capital. After product iteration, all became waste. This is exactly why our [72-hour rapid delivery system](/resources-en/rapid-delivery/72-hour-delivery-process-revealed) was designed for small batch flexibility.

### 🔍 Deep Analysis of the MOQ Trap
Traditional factory MOQ limits hide three business logics:
1. **Equipment Setup Cost Amortization** - 2-4 hours setup time per product change
2. **Material Procurement Batches** - Raw material suppliers also have MOQ requirements  
3. **Profit Maximization** - Large batches needed for adequate profit margins

### ✅ Geppetto Solution: Zero MOQ Smart Manufacturing
Our **AI Pre-Analysis System** completely changes this game:

**Technical Breakthroughs**:
- **Smart Process Path Optimization**: AI pre-plans optimal machining sequences, reducing 95% of setup time
- **Flexible Production Scheduling**: Multiple small-batch projects intelligently combined for maximum equipment utilization
- **Transparent Cost Accounting**: Real costs enable profitability from single pieces, no MOQ subsidies needed

**Real Results**:
- **Start from 1 piece**: True zero MOQ, seamless from prototype to small batch
- **Cost transparency**: Itemized pricing with no hidden fees
- **Cash flow friendly**: On-demand production, avoiding inventory pile-ups

## Pitfall #6: Intellectual Property Protection Gray Zone

### 💀 Traditional Trap Scenario
"Worried about design leaks, but factory says it's industry practice, no way to guarantee complete confidentiality..."

**IP Leak Risk Points**:
- **Drawing circulation**: Loose internal management, drawings may be copied
- **Process leaks**: Special processes learned by other clients
- **Supplier networks**: Upstream material suppliers obtain product information
- **Employee mobility**: Technical staff job-hopping with client information

### ✅ Geppetto IP Protection System

**Three-Level Confidentiality Mechanism**:
1. **Legal Level**: Strict NDA agreements with clear legal responsibilities
2. **Technical Level**: Encrypted drawing transmission, dedicated server storage
3. **Management Level**: Project team isolation, controlled information access

**Specific Protection Measures**:
- **Drawing Watermark Technology**: Every drawing has unique identification
- **Segmented Processing**: Critical processes distributed across different operations
- **Supplier Screening**: All partners sign confidentiality agreements
- **Regular Audits**: Third-party security company periodic audits
